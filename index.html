Cluster-State Service and Capacity-Aware Routing

CADDi Inc. · Tokyo

Real-time view of workflow execution across Kubernetes clusters, used to admit and route every workflow on the platform.

  • Hybrid design: a kube-rs watcher streams workflow events for low latency, and a consolidator polls every 250 ms to repair missed events. Workflow-state change events are published to Pub/Sub and consumed by other teams.
  • Per-cluster, per-lane capacity bands plus per-tenant concurrency limits, with an inflight cache (TTL + rollback) that closes the gap between submission and observation.
  • Failed placements report an explicit reason (concurrency limit reached, lane saturated, state unavailable).
  • High availability through leader election, multiple replicas and zone anti-affinity. Rolled out dark before cutover, replacing the previous snapshot-based component.

Automated Tenant Data Deletion (Data-Retention Enforcement)

CADDi Inc. · Tokyo

Irreversible, cross-team deletion of customer data on a durable-execution platform.

  • Control plane to data plane over Temporal Nexus, with parallel fan-out to 7 downstream services and serialized execution where services share a database.
  • Distributed authentication: scoped machine-to-machine JWTs propagated across hops and validated by every worker. Tenant identity is taken only from token claims, never from request input.
  • Idempotent at every layer (deterministic workflow IDs, conflict-tolerant calls) so recovery is a safe re-run. A shared SLO time budget across dependent services.
  • OpenTelemetry tracing across all services, and failure-path tests including simulated control-plane outages.
  • Two-tier SLOs: customer-facing (deletion within 70 days) and per-service (≤2 days, ≥99% success).
